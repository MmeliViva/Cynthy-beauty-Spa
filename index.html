About Us

Your Best Spa, Cynthy Beauty Spa

Welcome to Cynthy Beauty Spa, your destination for beautiful, well-groomed nails. We specialise in manicures, pedicures, gel nails, acrylic gels, Bio Sculpture strength and structure, sculptured nails, creative nail art, soak-offs and nail repairs. Whether you prefer a natural, elegant look or a bold, creative design, we are here to bring your nail vision to life with care, precision and attention to detail.
